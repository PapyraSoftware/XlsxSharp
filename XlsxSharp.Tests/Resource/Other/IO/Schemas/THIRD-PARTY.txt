The .xlsx/.xlsm/.xltx/.xltm files in this directory (and in Robustness/ and OpenXmlSdkCorpus/) are
test assets taken from https://github.com/dotnet/Open-XML-SDK, licensed under the MIT license.
They are real, Excel-authored files the Open XML SDK itself uses to exercise markup-compatibility
handling, extLst/ext content, a missing calcChain relationship, and general robustness against
real-world files (its own "OFCAT" corpus, plus the rest of its test asset library covering pivot
tables, charts, OLE/ActiveX objects, external links, web extensions and Office templates) - see
RealWorldFixtureTests.cs for how XlsxSharp uses them, including its documented, individually
investigated list of the few that are known not to work yet.

OpenXmlSdkCorpus/ mirrors the SDK's own assets/ directory layout, minus files whose content is
byte-for-byte identical to one already kept under a different name or location.
